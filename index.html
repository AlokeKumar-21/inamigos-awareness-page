About InAmigos Foundation

InAmigos Foundation is a youth-driven non-profit organization dedicated to building a better society through impactful social initiatives. Through education, skill development, environmental sustainability, community welfare, and empowerment programs, the organization strives to bring meaningful change to lives across India.

Our Key Projects

Project SEVA

Providing food, clothing, and essential support to underprivileged communities.

BACHPANSHALA

Promoting quality education, literacy, and digital learning opportunities for children.

JEEV

Supporting animal welfare through feeding, rescue, and awareness initiatives.

UDAAN

Empowering women through leadership, awareness, and skill development programs.

PRAKRITI

Encouraging environmental conservation through tree plantation and sustainability drives.

VIKAS

Enhancing employability through internship opportunities and career development programs.
